# supplier handbook v3.md

Fictional teaching document.

## 4.1 [A-TERMS]

Standard supplier payment terms are 45 days after a valid invoice is received. A signed supplier contract can specify different terms. For Nova, the signed contract specifies 30 days. Do not use the standard term when a contract exception applies.

## 4.2 [A-INVOICE]

Invoices must include a purchase order number and evidence of receipt. Missing purchase order numbers require clarification with the buyer. The assistant cannot certify delivery or invent a missing purchase order number.

## 2.1 [A-ONBOARD]

A new supplier must submit its company registration, tax identifier and verified bank details to procurement. Bank-account changes require an independent callback using the previously verified contact number.

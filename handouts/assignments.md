# Assignments

Three short individual assignments, about 45 minutes each, done after the day's session. Submit in the course channel
(or as your trainer asks) before 09:15 the next morning. Written feedback is given the same morning.
**Weight:** 15% of the course assessment (5% each).

## A1 (after Day 1): Model choice note
**Use case:** an internal assistant answering HR policy questions for 8,000 employees of an Indian bank. HR data must
stay in India.
**Deliver (one page):** options considered (at least three), weighted criteria with your weights, your choice, and the
one risk you would watch most closely.
**Scored on:** realistic options (1), criteria fit the use case (2), clear reasoning for the choice (2).

## A2 (after Day 2): RAG risks for your own use case
**Deliver (one page):** a knowledge-assistant use case from your work, and its **five biggest RAG risks**, each with a
concrete control. Example risks: outdated documents, permission leaks, scanned tables, ambiguous questions, Hindi or
mixed-language queries, answers that combine two documents.
**Scored on:** risks specific to the use case (2), controls concrete and feasible (2), one risk you would test first and how (1).

## A3 (after Day 4): ADR for your capstone
**Deliver:** one ADR using [adr_template.md](adr_template.md), for the decision your capstone team finds hardest.
**Scored on:** real options (1), honest consequences, including the downside you accept (2), how you would know the
decision was wrong (2).

# purchasing manual v2.md

Fictional teaching document.

## 3.1 [A-APPROVE]

AsterWorks purchases up to INR 50,000 require the department manager. Purchases above INR 50,000 require the finance controller. Splitting one purchase into smaller requests to avoid approval is prohibited.

## 3.2 [A-SOFTWARE]

Software purchases require an information-security review before approval, regardless of amount. Trial subscriptions that handle company information are included. An assistant may prepare a purchase draft, but may not approve it.

## 3.3 [A-EXPEDITE]

Urgent purchases still require the same approval route. The emergency approver is the duty finance controller when the usual approver is unavailable. An urgent label never grants an AI assistant approval authority.
